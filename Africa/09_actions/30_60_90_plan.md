# 30 / 60 / 90 Day Action Plan — Africa Market Entry
**Firm:** Canadian Data/IT Consultancy (bilingual EN/FR)
**Start date:** June 2026
**Goal:** First signed contract within 90 days; $400K–$700K in pipeline by month 12

---

## Overview

Partnership is the entry strategy. A new Canadian firm without prior African references will not win prime contracts on its own in the first 12 months. Every action below is calibrated to that reality.

**Three priorities in parallel:**
1. **Register everywhere** → become discoverable and eligible
2. **Partner fast** → get on proposal rosters before the next wave of bids closes
3. **Bid smart** → submit on small, winnable contracts to build references

---

## Key Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| No local partner found in time for bids | Start DRC/Côte d'Ivoire partner search in Week 1 alongside registrations |
| Prime contractor rosters are closed | Contact 9 primes in parallel, not sequentially; identify 3–4 backup primes |
| UNFPA/AfDB opportunities already closed | Verify status this week; if closed, build equivalent position for next wave |
| Donor-funding volatility (PEPFAR-style cuts) | Diversify across WB, AfDB, EU, GIZ, UNICEF — avoid single-donor dependency |
| Team bandwidth for proposal writing | Identify freelance proposal writer (francophone) as flex resource |
| Rate competitiveness | Validate against WB/AfDB indicative budgets in procurement plans before first proposal |
