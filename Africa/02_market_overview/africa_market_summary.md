# Africa Digital, Data & AI Consulting — Market Summary
**Firm:** Canadian Data/IT Consultancy (bilingual EN/FR)
**Date:** 24 May 2026 (Q2 2026)
**Scope:** Africa — priority: Francophone Africa, East Africa, Horn of Africa, West Africa

---

## 1. Market Context

Africa's digital and data landscape is at an inflection point. Donor commitments, continental policy frameworks, and national digital strategies are converging into concrete, procurable consulting assignments. For a small bilingual Canadian firm, the window of entry is now — before larger primes fully consolidate sub-contractor rosters for 2026–2030 program cycles.

### Five Key Market Findings

| # | Finding | Confidence |
|---|---------|------------|
| 1 | **Data governance is the single hottest cross-cutting theme** in African public-sector digital transformation. The €60M EU/AU "Data Governance in Africa" initiative, the AU Data Policy Framework, and national data strategies in Senegal, Rwanda, Côte d'Ivoire, Nigeria all converge here. | High |
| 2 | **Francophone Africa has a clear, identifiable funding pipeline** through AFD, Expertise France, Enabel, and GIZ francophone AU programs, plus World Bank's WARDIP2 ($137M) and DRC Digital Transformation ($500M). Bilingual capability is a genuine differentiator. | High |
| 3 | **AI governance has shifted from policy-writing to implementation** (2025–26). 49 African countries endorsed the Kigali AI Declaration. Most countries lack in-house implementation capacity. Most AI implementation work has not yet been procured. | High |
| 4 | **Health (DHIS2/HMIS) and Education MIS remain stable, recurring procurement categories** through UNICEF, WHO, UNFPA, and Global Fund implementing partners. Africa CDC has named DHIS2 as the continental priority for 2026. | High |
| 5 | **Canada is eligible to bid on World Bank and AfDB contracts** as a non-regional member. The constraint is references, not eligibility. Fastest path: subcontracting under established primes. | High |

## 2. Priority Market Segments

### Tier A — Pursue Aggressively

**Segment A1 — National and Regional Data Governance**
- Fit-to-services: 10/10 | Bilingual edge: High
- Active: EU/AU Data Governance in Africa Initiative (€60M), AU Data Policy Framework rollout, World Bank West Africa Data Hosting study, national strategies in Senegal, Rwanda, Côte d'Ivoire
- Contracting via: Enabel, GIZ, Expertise France, ESTDEV, World Bank STEP

**Segment A2 — Government Digital Transformation / GovTech**
- Active pipelines: DRC Digital Transformation ($500M, WB+AFD), WARDIP2 ($137M, WB), Madagascar Digital Governance, Côte d'Ivoire SDSI 2026–2030 (CFA 10.156B, 44 projects)
- Contracting via: World Bank STEP, UNDP eTendering, national PIUs

**Segment A3 — AI Readiness, Responsible AI & Data-Protection Frameworks**
- Demand: AI readiness assessments for ministries, responsible-AI frameworks, sector-specific AI pilots
- Countries with finalized national AI strategies (April 2026): South Africa, Côte d'Ivoire, Rwanda, Senegal, Nigeria, Benin
- Most implementation work not yet procured → entry window open

### Tier B — Pursue Selectively

**Segment B4 — Health Data Systems (DHIS2/HMIS + Interoperability)**
- Recurring procurement by UNICEF, WHO, UNFPA, Global Fund
- Entry requires DHIS2 certification + HISP node partnership
- Active examples: Madagascar HMIS, Zanzibar HMIS, Egypt digital health, Mozambique SRH

**Segment B5 — M&E Systems for Donor-Funded Programs**
- Predictable, repeating category; low barriers for a bilingual boutique
- Buyers: USAID (transition), FCDO, EU DG INTPA, AFD, GIZ, Global Fund, Gavi

**Segment B6 — Education MIS (EMIS) and Skills Data**
- Lower deal volume than health, but lower competition
- Entry via Expertise France RTIA (€46M, 2024–2027) expert pool

### Tier C — Watch / Partner-Only Entry

- Financial inclusion / Central bank modernization (dominated by Big 4)
- Port and logistics modernization (data is a small lot within EPC contracts)
- Agriculture data and digitalization (heavy local-firm involvement)

## 4. Key Buyers by Category

### Multilateral Funders
- **World Bank (IBRD/IDA):** DE4A program, STEP system, eConsult2 — Canada fully eligible
- **African Development Bank (AfDB):** €10.47B 2026 procurement spend; E-Consultant registration required
- **UNDP:** African Digital Transformation Solutions Forum; EOI-based entry
- **UNICEF:** MICS Plus LTA mechanism; UNGM registration required
- **WHO + Africa CDC:** DHIS2 continental public-health mandate

### EU / Member-State Agencies
- **Expertise France:** D4D Hub, RTIA (€46M), expanding DRC/Ethiopia presence
- **AFD:** Co-financier DRC digital ($500M); calls at afd.fr
- **Enabel:** Lead implementer of €60M Data Governance in Africa initiative
- **GIZ:** DataCipation programme, FAIR Forward AI, DTC Kenya/Rwanda

### Continental / Pan-African
- **Smart Africa Alliance:** 32 member states, SADA capacity-building procurement
- **AUDA-NEPAD:** Continental implementation arm
- **African Union Commission:** AU Data Policy Framework rollout

### Priority National Governments
- Côte d'Ivoire MEPD, Senegal Min. Digital Economy, Rwanda MINICT + NISR, DRC MPTNTIC, Nigeria FMCIDE, Kenya MICDE
