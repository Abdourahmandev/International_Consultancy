## Executive Summary

### Top 5 findings

1. **Data governance is now the single hottest cross-cutting theme in African public-sector digital transformation.** The €60M EU/AU "Data Governance in Africa" initiative (Enabel, GIZ, Expertise France, ESTDEV, HAUS, Digital Africa), the AU Data Policy Framework rollout, national data strategies in Senegal, Rwanda, Côte d'Ivoire, Nigeria, and active World Bank work like "Data hosting and Governance in West Africa" all converge here. This is our highest-fit, highest-volume segment.

2. **Francophone Africa has a clear, identifiable funding pipeline** through AFD, Expertise France, Enabel (Belgium), and GIZ's francophone AU programs — and a parallel pipeline through the World Bank's WARDIP2 ($137M, Benin/Liberia/Sierra Leone) and DRC Digital Transformation Project ($500M with WB + AFD). Bilingual capability is a real differentiator here; most North American competitors don't have it.

3. **AI governance has shifted from policy-writing to implementation** in 2025–26. The Africa AI Declaration (49 countries, Kigali), the AU Continental AI Strategy 2025–2030, the announced $60B Africa AI Fund and Africa AI Council, plus finalized national AI strategies (South Africa April 2026, Côte d'Ivoire, Rwanda, Senegal, Nigeria, Benin) create downstream demand for responsible-AI frameworks, AI readiness assessments, and sector-specific AI deployments. Most of this work has not been procured yet.

4. **Health (DHIS2/HMIS) and education MIS systems remain a stable, recurring procurement category** through UNICEF, WHO, UNFPA, and Global Fund implementing partners, with a clear technical stack (DHIS2 + interoperability + dashboards). Africa CDC has explicitly named DHIS2 as the priority tool for continental public-health digitization in 2026.

5. **Canada is an eligible non-regional member of both the World Bank Group and the African Development Bank.** Canadian firms can bid directly on both. The realistic constraint is not eligibility — it's track record and references. The fastest path to credibility is subcontracting under established primes (GFA, Tetra Tech, Adam Smith International, Palladium, IBF, Particip) or partnering with local francophone firms.

### Top 5 recommended actions (next 30 days)

1. **Register on five procurement platforms this week**: UNGM, World Bank STEP/SPN, AfDB Data Portal (ADC) E-Consultant, UNDP eTendering, and EU Funding & Tenders portal. Set up daily email alerts on keywords: data governance, digital transformation, business intelligence, MIS, M&E, data warehouse, interoperability, AI strategy.

2. **Submit an EOI on the next AfDB or World Bank data-governance assignment that appears in Francophone Africa or East Africa**, even as a junior partner. The objective in the first 90 days is reference contracts, not perfect ones.

3. **Build a one-page capability statement in English + French**, anchored on three offers: (a) data governance assessment and roadmap, (b) BI/dashboard implementation, (c) AI readiness and responsible-AI framework. Use this as the door-opener for all subsequent outreach.

4. **Open conversations with 3–5 prime contractors** (GFA Consulting Group, Tetra Tech, Palladium, Particip, IBF International, Adam Smith International). Position as a francophone-capable, Canadian-domiciled technical sub for data and AI lots in their proposals to AfDB, WB, EU, AFD.

5. **Engage Trade Commissioner Service (TCS) and Global Affairs Canada** for IFI procurement support — Canada actively subsidizes Canadian firms pursuing AfDB/WB/UN business, including market intelligence and pre-positioning support. This is free and underused.

## Priority Market Segments

Segments are ranked by fit-to-firm × volume × accessibility for a new Canadian entrant.

### Tier A — Pursue aggressively

**1. National and regional data governance** *(highest priority)*
The EU/AU Data Governance in Africa Initiative (€60M, 2023–) is actively contracting through Enabel, GIZ, Expertise France, ESTDEV. National data strategies are at the *implementation* stage in Senegal, Rwanda, Côte d'Ivoire and at the *drafting* stage in many AU member states. World Bank just procured "Data hosting and Governance in West Africa" covering Benin, Cabo Verde, Gambia, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, Liberia. Fit-to-services: 10/10. Bilingual edge: high.

**2. Government digital transformation & GovTech**
The AU Digital Transformation Strategy for Africa 2020–2030 is the umbrella. Concrete pipelines: DRC Digital Transformation Project ($500M, WB+AFD), WARDIP2 ($137M, WB), Madagascar Digital Governance & ID, Côte d'Ivoire MEPD SDSI 2026–2030 (CFA 10.156B, 44 projects). UNDP Resilience Hub for Africa is running the African Digital Transformation Solutions Forum with active EOIs.

**3. AI readiness, responsible AI & data-protection frameworks**
49 African countries endorsed the Africa Declaration on AI (Kigali). Africa AI Council launched November 2025. South Africa finalized AI policy April 2026. Demand: AI readiness assessments for ministries, responsible-AI / risk frameworks, sector AI pilots (agri, health, education, public services), and operationalization of national AI strategies. Most countries do not yet have implementation capacity in-house.

### Tier B — Pursue selectively

**4. Health data systems (DHIS2/HMIS, interoperability)**
DHIS2 is the de facto standard, endorsed by Africa CDC for 2026. UNICEF, WHO, UNFPA, Global Fund regularly procure DHIS2 configuration, dashboarding, interoperability, capacity-building. Madagascar HMIS, Zanzibar HMIS dashboarding, Egypt digital health transformation, Mozambique SRH program, Malawi climate-health platform are active examples. Need DHIS2-certified specialists; partner with HISP nodes (HISP Tanzania, HISP West Africa, HISP UiO) to enter.

**5. M&E systems for donor-funded programs**
Donors (USAID transitions, FCDO, EU DG INTPA, AFD, GIZ, Global Fund, Gavi, Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation) need M&E dashboards, results-framework systems, and data-quality work for almost every multi-year program. Predictable, repeating procurement category.

**6. Education MIS (EMIS) and skills data**
Lesotho LEMIS (UNICEF + HISP, DHIS2-based) is the current reference. Comparable EMIS modernization is on the agenda in many countries through GPE, World Bank education projects, and AFD. Smaller deals than health, but lower competition.

### Tier C — Watch / partner-only entry

**7. Financial inclusion, fintech data, central bank modernization**
Strong demand — but dominated by Big 4 consulting, Oliver Wyman, McKinsey, and specialized RegTech firms. Realistic entry only via subcontracting on data-engineering lots or via a francophone West Africa central-bank angle (BCEAO, BEAC).

**8. Port and logistics modernization**
Real opportunities (Lomé, Dakar, Abidjan, Mombasa, Djibouti, Lobito Corridor) but typically wrapped into large EPC contracts where data is a small lot. Pursue only as sub to an engineering prime (Egis, Artelia, COWI).

**9. Agriculture data & digitalization**
AfDB Feed Africa, GIZ green-digital programs, IFAD digital agriculture. Heavy local-firm involvement; treat as opportunistic.
